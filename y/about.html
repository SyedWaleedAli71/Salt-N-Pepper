Our Journey

Our Story

Salt'n Pepper began with a simple but powerful belief — that every meal should be an experience worth remembering. Founded in Lahore, our first restaurant quickly became a landmark for families, professionals and food lovers seeking something beyond the ordinary.

Over the years, we have grown from a single dining destination into a family of brands spanning restaurants, express outlets and village-style venues across Pakistan. Each location carries the same spirit: exceptional food, attentive service and an atmosphere that makes every guest feel genuinely welcome.

From intimate family dinners to grand celebrations, Salt'n Pepper has been part of countless unforgettable moments — and we are proud to continue that tradition every single day.
